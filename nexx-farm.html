NEXX Farm

Auto farm.
Auto donate.

NEXX Farm plays one Clash of Clans account that is already logged in on the phone. It attacks with your army, then donates to your clan, and keeps looping until you stop it. No root needed.

Download NEXX Farm How to set it up
1 · Army

Your army, from a link

Paste a copy-army link once. The bot saves it in the game and uses it every run. It never picks troops for you.

2 · Attack

Attacks by sight

It finds a base, drops the army and ends the battle at the damage you choose (default 50 %), then goes home for the next one.

3 · Donate

Fills requests fast

Between attacks it donates to every request in clan chat, in your private clan or a busy request clan.

Two ways to donate
  • Private clanYour own accounts stay in the clan and request; the bot fills each request from the chat.
  • Request clanMany players join, request and leave within seconds. The bot taps the newest request the moment it shows.
  • Until the loot runs outOptional: donate with no count until the elixir is low, then attack to refill it, then donate again.
Runs on its own
  • Until you stop itOr switch on Running hours (1 to 24) and it stops by itself.
  • Wall upgradesOptional: before its attacks it spends the gold and elixir on walls. Adds 5 coins per 24 h, and works only on a 16:9 screen (for example 1280 × 720).
  • Pop-ups clearedGame windows are closed the same way NEXX Bot does it.
  • Updates itselfA new version installs when the app opens; a run cut off by it carries on after.

  • An English Clash of Clans account, logged in on that phone
